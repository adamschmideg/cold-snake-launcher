Privacy Policy — Cold Snake

Last updated: 2026-08-25

Cold Snake does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. It does not require an internet connection to function.

Cold Snake requests the following device permission:

  • Set alarm (com.android.alarm.permission.SET_ALARM) — used only to open your device's clock/alarm app when you tap the Clock tile in dumb mode. No alarm data is read, stored, or sent anywhere by Cold Snake itself.

If Cold Snake later adds features that involve data collection (e.g. optional session score history), this policy will be updated before that change ships, and the update will be described plainly here.
